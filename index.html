All-electron calculations

All data in this section are from calculations using all-electron methods.

Note: In those calculations, Na and Li specially use core-valence correlation basis sets: for aug-cc/ano-pV(D+d)Z the Na/Li basis is aug-cc-pCVDZ, for aug-cc/ano-pV(T+d)Z it is aug-cc/ano-pCVTZ, etc. The aug-cc-pCVQZ of Li is used for 5Z basis set calculation due to the lack of higher-order basis sets. Because the s-valence orbital of Na and Li has non-negligible correlation with their p-core orbitals, which can not be captured by regular valence basis sets. The comparison of different basis sets for Na and Li is shown in the Li/Na Basis Comparison.

Comparison across methods

Combines ECP and all-electron SVD-projected results on a shared axis. ECP xTC is shown in orange, AE-Dunning (SVD, aug-cc-pV(X+d)Z) in green, AE-ANO (SVD, aug-ano-pV(X+d)Z) in purple, F12-Dunning (Diagonal ansatz, F12b, and aug-cc-pV(X+d)Z) in red, and plain frozen-core CCSD(T) in blue. The convergence plot uses a unified basis axis (pvDZ, avDZ, pvTZ, avTZ, pvQZ, avQZ, pv5Z, av5Z): xTC occupies the augmented slots, plain frozen-core CCSD(T) occupies the non-augmented slots. The per-basis plots include F12 at the matching avDZ/avTZ/avQZ cardinal and plain CCSD(T) at pvDZ/pvTZ/pvQZ/pv5Z as fixed reference.
