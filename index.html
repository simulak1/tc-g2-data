All-electron calculations

All data in this section are from calculations using all-electron methods. Na and Li use core-valence correlation basis sets: for aug-cc/ano-pV(D+d)Z the Na/Li basis is aug-cc-pCVDZ, for aug-cc/ano-pV(T+d)Z it is aug-cc/ano-pCVTZ, etc., because the s-valence orbital of Na and Li has non-negligible correlation energy not captured by regular valence basis sets.
